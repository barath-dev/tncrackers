# Diwali offer imagery — Gemini prompt pack

Ready-to-paste prompts for Gemini's image models (**Gemini 2.5 Flash Image**,
aka "Nano Banana," for fast iteration and good prompt adherence; **Imagen 4**
for the highest-fidelity photographic/product shots). Organized by where
each image would go on the site, with the Diwali-offer imagery first since
that's the priority.

## How to use these

- **Reuse the style anchor** below in every prompt — it's what keeps the
  whole set feeling like one brand instead of 15 random images. Every
  prompt already includes it; if you tweak a prompt, keep this line intact.
- **Pick the model by job**: Nano Banana for anything painterly/atmospheric
  (banners, mood shots) where you'll iterate a few times; Imagen 4 for
  clean product shots (gift boxes) where fidelity matters more than speed.
- **Don't bake sale text into the image.** Text rendering in these models
  is still unreliable (misspellings, warped letterforms), and the site
  already overlays real HTML text with proper fonts/colors on top of photo
  backgrounds (see the hero section). Generate clean, text-free background
  art and let CSS do the typography — it'll always look sharper. A couple
  of prompts below have an optional "with text" variant for off-site use
  (WhatsApp status, Instagram) where a baked-in headline is more normal;
  even there, ask for short text and expect to regenerate a few times.
- **Aspect ratio**: set it via the tool's aspect-ratio control rather than
  describing it in words — models honor an explicit setting far more
  reliably than a sentence like "16:9."
- **Always add a negative/exclusion line** (last line of every prompt
  below) — it measurably reduces watermarks, garbled logos, and mangled
  hands.
- These models don't produce true alpha-channel transparency. For the
  "sticker/badge" prompt, ask for a plain white or solid-color backdrop and
  cut it out afterward (Preview's instant alpha, remove.bg, or Photoshop).

**Style anchor** (used throughout):
> Warm, premium editorial photography for an Indian festival fireworks
> brand. Palette: deep indigo-black night sky (#17141C), burnt ember-orange
> light (#B54B1F), a hint of dusty plum (#5B3E6B), antique brass accents
> (#A9863C). Cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field, high detail. Not
> garish, not neon, not a stock-photo cliché.

## A. Diwali offer & sale imagery (priority)

### A1 — Homepage hero, Diwali edition (16:9)
> [style anchor] A wide cinematic shot of a warm terracotta courtyard at
> night, foreground lined with rows of small lit clay diyas (oil lamps),
> soft golden bokeh from distant string lights, two or three aerial
> fireworks shells bursting softly in brass-gold and dusty plum high in a
> deep indigo sky over rooftops in the far background. The upper two-thirds
> of the frame stay dark and uncluttered for text to sit on top of later.
> No people in close-up, no text, no logos, no watermark.

### A2 — "This Diwali" offer banner, text-free (21:9 ultra-wide)
> [style anchor] A horizontal banner background: a row of lit clay diyas
> along the bottom edge, soft-focus marigold petals and a few drifting gold
> sparks, set against a gradient from deep indigo on the left to warm
> ember-orange on the right. Generous empty, uncluttered space in the
> center-left third of the frame for bold sale text to be added afterward.
> No text, no numbers, no watermark, no logos.

### A3 — Instagram/WhatsApp offer post, text-free (1:1 square)
> [style anchor] A top-down flat-lay of a small brass diya, a scatter of
> marigold petals, and a handful of unlit sparklers arranged on a dark
> charcoal surface, warm directional light from one side, plenty of empty
> dark space in the upper third of the frame for a headline to be added
> afterward. No text, no watermark, no logos.

### A4 — WhatsApp status / Instagram story, text-free (9:16 vertical)

### A5 — Discount badge / sticker asset (isolated cutout)

### A6 — "Offer ends soon" urgency banner background (16:9)

### A7 — Email/newsletter header (3:1 wide strip)

## Negative / exclusion line to append to every prompt

> No text, no watermark, no signature, no logo, no brand names, no
> distorted hands, no extra fingers, no blurry artifacts, not
> photorealistic humans in close-up unless specified.

## After generating

Drop finished images into `public/images/diwali/` (wide/ and card/
subfolders, matching the existing convention in `public/images/`), keep
the file sizes reasonable (compress the same way the current photos were:
`sips -s format jpeg -s formatOptions <60-75> --resampleWidth <640|1600>`),
and if any generated image ends up looking too close to a real product,
brand, or person, regenerate rather than ship it.
